Deleting your MealMate account

You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the app:

  1. Open MealMate.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This immediately and permanently removes your profile, address, budget, saved stores, meal plans, shopping lists, blocked recipes, price corrections and contributed price observations. It cannot be undone.

If you can no longer sign in, email alisohail6835@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.
