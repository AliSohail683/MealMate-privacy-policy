MealMate — Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

MealMate plans meals around your food budget. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. It is deliberately short, because the app collects very little.

What we store

DataWhy
Your account identifierCreated when you sign in with Apple or Google, so your plan follows you to a new device.
Your name, if the provider supplies oneTo greet you in the app. Optional; the app works without it.
The address you chooseTo set your currency, local price levels, and find nearby supermarkets.
Your budget and household sizeTo build a plan that fits the money.
Your meal plans and shopping listsSo they are there when you open the app.
Prices you correctTo price your plan accurately, and — anonymously — to help other shoppers in your country.

What we do not collect

No analytics. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites. No contacts, photos, browsing history, or health data. We do not sell data, and there is no advertising SDK in the app.

Location

If you tap “Use my location”, the coordinates are sent once to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to turn them into an address, and to the Overpass service to find nearby shops. We store only the address you then choose — never a location history, and never in the background.

Prices you contribute

When you correct an ingredient’s price, that correction is saved to your account and also recorded as one anonymous observation: the ingredient, the price, the currency, the country, and optionally the shop. It is published to other users only as a median, and only once at least two people have reported the same item.

Your identity is not attached to it in any way another user can reach. The account identifier on those rows is protected by database column permissions, so neither the app nor any other user can read who reported a price.

Third-party services

ServiceWhat it receives

Apple / Google Sign-InAuthenticates you and returns an account identifier.
OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass)An address or coordinates, to look up places. No account data.
TheMealDBRecipe requests only. No personal data.
Open Food Facts / Open PricesIngredient and category lookups. No personal data.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, saved stores, plans, shopping lists, blocked recipes, price corrections, and your contributed price observations. It cannot be undone, and it happens immediately — there is no waiting period and nothing is retained.

Children

MealMate is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and the new version will be published here.
